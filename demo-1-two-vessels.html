Endocrine · Thyroid disorders

A 24-year-old woman comes to the clinic because of 3 months of palpitations, heat intolerance, and a 4-kg weight loss despite an increased appetite. Pulse 110/min, respirations 18/min, BP 132/68 mm Hg. Examination shows mild proptosis, a diffuse, non-tender thyroid, and a fine tremor. Laboratory studies show a suppressed TSH and an elevated free T4.

Which of the following additional findings is most likely in this patient?

ABradycardia on cardiac examination
BDecreased deep tendon reflexes
CLid lag and a stare on downward gaze
DWeight gain despite the increased appetite
EProlonged, heavy menstrual cycles
Why A is wrong: thyroid hormone sensitizes the heart to catecholamines — the sympathetic drive goes up, producing tachycardia and a widened pulse pressure. The rate never falls while T4 is high.
