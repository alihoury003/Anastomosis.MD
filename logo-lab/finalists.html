My read.

The two families. V2A and V5 are the calm wave — steady, breathing, confident. V2D, V2E, V4A and V4B are the heartbeat — one moment of drama in a calm line. The bell question is now really a question of personality: the flat ring (V2A) is quiet and geometric; the elliptical disc (V2D/V2E) adds subtle depth; the drum (V4A/V4B/V5) is the sculptural one you kept coming back to — it finally reads unmistakably as a stethoscope.

V5 is the synthesis. It carries the exact bell you asked for, on the exact wave you loved — the first mark that is fully yours, with no compromise on either component. If the bell feels heavy against the calm wave, V4A is the same bell with the spike giving the mark a second focal point; if the drum is too literal, V2E is the elegant one-colour minimal take.

The 16 px truth. Whatever wins, the spike (if any) will dominate at favicon size and the bell will shrink to a blob — that is normal and fixable: the production mark gets a dedicated simplified 16 px cut (spike amplitude compressed ≈25%, bell reduced to its silhouette). Every finalist here survives that treatment.

My call, if you want it: V5 — the calm wave with the drum bell — with V4A as the close second if you want the ECG energy. Two colours (V2D) is beautiful but busier; one ink is the premium move.

After you pick, the rollout is: browser-tab favicon on every page, the loading animation inspired by the mark (noted for later), social avatars, and the mark replaces the old wrench in nav, footer and the landing page's part-6 screenshot.
