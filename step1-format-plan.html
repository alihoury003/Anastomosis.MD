The honest readWe're not first — and that's fine

Truth first: this news is five months old. AMBOSS sessions and UWorld self-assessments already match the new block length. Anyone claiming "first to adapt" today would be lying — and medical students fact-check everything.
But the real gap is still wide open: most students still train on 40-question blocks out of default inertia; several major prep pages still describe the old format (Lecterio's own FAQ still says "seven 60-minute blocks… up to 40 questions" — months after the change); and nobody owns the position that combines new-format-native defaults with every distractor explained. That intersection is exactly us.
