Before you click buyThe 7-point checklist

  • The checkout price is the truth. It should read ~$49–51. If it suddenly shows hundreds of dollars, the registry has flagged the name "premium" — stop, screenshot it, and bring it back to me.
  • You're on the standard country tier. The registry charges US/UK/Brazil-type buyers more; Lebanon is standard tier. (Another reason the price at checkout matters more than any list.)
  • Turn auto-renew ON + a calendar reminder 30 days before expiry. This one is critical: .md has no redemption period — 30 days after expiry the name is deleted outright and open to anyone. A missed renewal = the brand domain gone.
  • Don't panic if it's not instant. .md registrations can take from a few minutes up to ~2 days to activate (the registry processes payments manually in some flows). Not a scam — just a small registry.
  • WHOIS privacy: the .md registry doesn't really support it; Netim and Regery offer a free proxy — take it if offered. If not, your name/email sit in public whois (your contact email is already public on the site anyway).
  • Transfers don't add a year on .md (the registry keeps your expiry date), so there's no free-year trick — pick a good registrar now and stay.
  • Buy it this week. It's available today. $49 to own the brand's exact name — the cheapest, highest-leverage asset purchase this project will ever make.

Paying from LebanonWhat actually works

Credit/debit card (Visa/Mastercard) works at all the picks if your card is enabled for international online payments — that's the usual blocker with Lebanese banks.

PayPal is accepted by Netim and Regery if you have (or can get) an account funded.

Bitcoin is accepted by Regery and Netcetera — the fallback if cards fail. It's a normal payment rail for them, not a sketchy one.

Bank transfer — possible at most, but slow (registry advises 3+ banking days ahead) and painful from Lebanon. Card/PayPal/BTC first.
